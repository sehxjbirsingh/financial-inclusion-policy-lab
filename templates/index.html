💡 Key Takeaway for Everyday Citizens

In almost every country, the #1 reason people remain unbanked is not because they don't want an account, but because they believe they "don't earn enough money to qualify" (55%) or fear "hidden bank fees" (44%). Under modern regulations in most countries, basic digital accounts and mobile money wallets are legally required to be 100% free with zero minimum balance.

Methodological Notes & Responsible Policy Use

  • Descriptive Character: All clusters, indices, and simulated projections represent exploratory decision-support models. They identify statistical associations and peer patterns, not definitive causal laws.
  • Within-Country Disparities: National aggregates can mask sharp localized inequalities across rural areas, refugee communities, and marginalized socio-economic strata.
  • Preprocessing Rules: Features with greater than 35% missingness are automatically excluded in unsupervised cluster pipelines; remaining values use median imputation.
  • Empirical Elasticities: Policy simulation models are calibrated using empirical elasticity coefficients from World Bank policy research, reflecting diminishing marginal returns as access approaches universal saturation.
